matscipy.fracture_mechanics.continuation

Force-only pseudo-arclength tracing of clamped crack clusters (lattice-trapping S-curves).

A clamped cluster (clusters.clamped_cluster) in a SinclairCrack with variable_alpha=False, energy=”total” is the problem: free atoms move, clamped atoms sit on the K-field. The tracer works in x = (u, k) with k = K/K_G and u the free-atom displacements relative to the field; the arclength metric is ds^2 = |du|^2 + alpha^2 dk^2. Correctors use a surrogate Jacobian (the Hessian of any matscipy many-body potential, e.g. Stillinger-Weber for Si, plus X-H springs, scaled by one calibrated factor) with good-Broyden updates from every force call, then Newton-Krylov. For large clusters the surrogate can stay sparse, with the Broyden updates as a low-rank correction (LowRankJacobian, TraceSettings(surrogate=”sparse”)).

class matscipy.fracture_mechanics.continuation.LowRankJacobian(S, U=None, V=None, max_rank=120)

Bases: object

Corrector Jacobian B = S + U V^T for large clusters (TraceSettings(surrogate=”sparse”)).

S (nf x nf+1, scipy.sparse) is the surrogate Jacobian at the last refresh, U V^T (U: nf x m, V: nf+1 x m) the limited-memory good-Broyden correction learned from the true forces since, one column pair per update, compressed by truncated SVD to at most max_rank terms at each refresh. The bordered system [B; t] d = r is solved by block elimination on S’s free-free block S_u (one sparse LU per refresh, reused by the tangent, every corrector iteration and the Krylov preconditioner) and Sherman-Morrison-Woodbury for U V^T (S_u^-1 U cached), plus one step of iterative refinement, which guards the block elimination near folds, where S_u (a Hessian) is nearly singular. Exact algebra: the same B as the dense path, without the (3N)^2 storage and O(N^3) solves.

refresh(Jn, Js, mode='projected')

Merge a new surrogate Jacobian Jn (previous one Js) into B and restart the span.

“projected” adds Jn - Js only off the span Q: on Q, B already holds the true forces’ current secants, which contain that change, and adding it there too counts it twice per refresh (near a fold, where the critical curvature changes fast, this made the tangent retrace the stable branch). “carry” adds it everywhere (the behaviour before), “reset” sets B = Jn. A LowRankJacobian B takes the same rule on its low-rank part (Js is then its S).

matscipy.fracture_mechanics.continuation.trace_s_curve(problem, surrogate, k0, *, direction=1, settings=None, callback=None)

Trace the S-curve K(u) of a clamped crack cluster from load k0 = K/K_G.

The start (the problem’s geometry, moved rigidly with the field from its build load to k0) is relaxed at fixed k; then a tangent predictor (tangent; capped to dr_cap and, with max_advance, to that crack advance) and bordered Broyden corrector (Newton-Krylov after broyden_retries halvings), with the surrogate Jacobian refreshed (refresh) every surrogate_every points. The corrector Jacobian is dense or sparse plus low rank (surrogate; the surrogate’s jac may return either). If the problem has hvp(x, v), the exact directional derivative of the free-atom forces dF/dx(x) v (x and v in (u, k)), Newton-Krylov uses it instead of finite differences of the forces. Folds are sign changes of dk/ds (not at the first point after the start); the branch label starts “stable” and alternates at each fold. Stops after stop_after_folds folds plus extra_after_last_fold in k, on leaving k_window moving outward, at max_points, or when a step needs more than max_calls_per_point force calls or ds falls below ds_min (“stalled”). callback(frame) is called for every accepted point.

Returns (frames, status): extxyz-ready Atoms with arrays forces and info K_over_KG, K, energy, branch, point_index, method, ds, calls; status one of “folds”, “window”, “complete”, “stalled”.
