matscipy.fracture_mechanics.continuation

Force-only pseudo-arclength tracing of clamped crack clusters (lattice-trapping S-curves).

A clamped cluster (clusters.clamped_cluster) in a SinclairCrack with variable_alpha=False, energy=”total” is the problem: free atoms move, clamped atoms sit on the K-field. The tracer works in x = (u, k) with k = K/K_G and u the free-atom displacements relative to the field; the arclength metric is ds^2 = |du|^2 + alpha^2 dk^2. Correctors use a surrogate Jacobian (the Hessian of any matscipy many-body potential, e.g. Stillinger-Weber for Si, plus X-H springs, scaled by one calibrated factor) with good-Broyden updates from every force call, then Newton-Krylov.

matscipy.fracture_mechanics.continuation.trace_s_curve(problem, surrogate, k0, *, direction=1, settings=None, callback=None)

Trace the S-curve K(u) of a clamped crack cluster from load k0 = K/K_G.

The start (the problem’s geometry, moved rigidly with the field from its build load to k0) is relaxed at fixed k; then secant predictor (capped to dr_cap and, with max_advance, to that crack advance) and bordered Broyden corrector (Newton-Krylov after broyden_retries halvings), with the surrogate Jacobian refreshed every surrogate_every points. Folds are sign changes of dk/ds; the branch label starts “stable” and alternates at each fold. Stops after stop_after_folds folds plus extra_after_last_fold in k, on leaving k_window moving outward, at max_points, or when a step needs more than max_calls_per_point force calls or ds falls below ds_min (“stalled”). callback(frame) is called for every accepted point.

Returns (frames, status): extxyz-ready Atoms with arrays forces and info K_over_KG, K, energy, branch, point_index, method, ds, calls; status one of “folds”, “window”, “complete”, “stalled”.
